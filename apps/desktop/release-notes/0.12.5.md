## Fixed

- **A screen share with audio ending silenced the presenter** for everyone who had set that person's volume above 100 %, until the presenter switched channels or reconnected. The fix sits in the listener's client: the app dropped the presenter's level meter when the share's audio track ended, and the volume boost above 100 % hangs on that meter. Now only the microphone track's end drops it. So the people who could not hear you need this version, not you.

Nothing else changes; this version works with every server version.
