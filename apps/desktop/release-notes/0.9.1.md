## Improved

- **The share dialog opens at once.** Until now it only appeared after a preview picture of every open window had been made, which took several seconds with many windows. Now it opens right away, lists your windows and screens a moment later and fills in the preview pictures as they become ready. You can choose a window and share it without waiting for its picture.
- **Quick Share starts sooner.** It no longer waits for preview pictures it never shows (Windows).
