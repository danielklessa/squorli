## New

- **Report an account or a server to the operator of the directory.** "Report account" in the menu of a member or a friend reports a Squorli account, its name or its picture. "Report server" in the server rail's menu and in the server browser reports a whole server. The dialog of a reported channel message now asks "Who gets the report?": the moderators of the server as before, or the operator of the directory when the moderators do nothing or are the problem themselves. That second way sends the message along as evidence; the reported person does not learn who reported. Needs a current Squorli Directory (directory.squorli.com is one).
- **Notices about your account.** When the operator of the directory warns or suspends your Squorli account, or removes its name or picture, the app shows a notice with the reason, also while you are chatting. "Understood" marks it as read; the account page keeps earlier notices.
- **A suspended account says so.** While your Squorli account is suspended, the friends view explains it (until when, why, where to ask) instead of "No connection to the directory". Servers with an up-to-date version that refuse suspended accounts show the same on their page instead of a lost session. When the suspension ends, friends and servers are back without signing in again.
- **Servers the directory refused.** The app no longer connects to a chat server the operator of the directory has refused, not by its address either. Such a server stays in your server rail with a red mark and its page says what happened. "Remove from the list" takes it out of the rail on all your devices; a server that is allowed again comes back by itself.
