The radio's equalizer can be shown over the tiles, and on/off settings are toggle switches now. Works with every server version; Squorli Server 0.12.2 carries the same client for the browser.

## Improved

- **The equalizer over the tiles.** While the radio's equalizer is on, a switch right of its button in the stage's button bar draws it over the tiles instead of behind them. The tiles stay clickable. Off until you turn it on.
- **Toggle switches for on/off settings.** In the settings, in Administration and in the channel dialog every on/off setting is a switch now. Lists where several entries are ticked, such as a role's permissions, keep their checkboxes.
