## Improved

- **Your sign-in sessions are stored encrypted by the system** (Windows, or a keyring on Linux), like your account key already was, and no longer in plain text in the app's data folder. A computer without such a store keeps them where they were.
- **Direct message text stays out of notifications until you switch it on** (Settings > Sounds, "Show the text of direct messages in the notification"). Direct messages are end-to-end encrypted, but the system may keep a notification's text in plain text. Mentions in channels keep their own switch.
- **The role menu and the reports list show only what you may do.** A role you cannot hand out is no longer offered, and deleting messages from a report needs the permission to manage messages. Needs Squorli Server 0.8.4 or newer to match; older servers simply refuse what the old menu offered.
- **The rules of use are linked** in the notice about a moderator's measure and under the form for a Squorli account.
- **The debug panel hides your session token**, so a screenshot of it is safe to share.
