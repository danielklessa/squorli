## New

- **Notifications.** A direct message or a mention you do not see right now shows as a notification of the system; a click brings the window forward and opens the conversation or the channel. On by default; Settings > Sounds switches them off, sends a test and can leave the message text out.
- **Who watches your screen share.** While you share your screen, the avatars (or initials) of everybody watching it overlap in the top left corner of your share, on the stage and in its own window. Point at one to see it large with the name. Only you see this list.
- **Link previews in direct messages can be switched off** under Settings > View (with a Squorli account): no link you send is looked up, and you see no previews in the messages you receive. The choice follows your account, encrypted.

## Improved

- **The stage in its own window keeps its place.** The main window shows where the stage went, with "Bring the stage back here" and "Show the window".
- **The camera's background blur runs without loading anything from other sites**; model and program files ship with the app.
- **The licenses list** names the app's own packages too.

## Fixed

- **"Show the window" and "Show the stage" now bring the stage's window to the front** on Windows.
