The camera preview shows the background blur again, you can see your own picture unmirrored, a sound announces a new screen share, and you choose who may see which game you play. Works with every server version; the game rule needs Squorli Server 0.13.0 on the servers it shall apply to.

## New

- **Who may see my game.** Settings > Spiele has "Wer mein Spiel sehen darf": "Alle", "Nur ausgewählte" or "Alle außer", with a list you fill from your friends and the members of your servers, or by a right click on a person ("Mein Spiel verbergen", "Mein Spiel zeigen"). The Directory and the servers hand your game only to the people the rule allows; for that they receive the keys of the listed people with each game report and do not store them. Your lists follow your account, encrypted. A server on a version before 0.13.0 learns nothing about your game while a rule applies; the settings name it.
- **A sound for a new screen share.** When somebody else in your voice channel starts sharing their screen, three short rising notes play. Its switch and preview are in Settings > Töne.
- **Camera preview in the settings.** Settings > Kamera shows your picture with the chosen background after "Vorschau starten".
- **Your own picture unmirrored.** "Mein Kamerabild gespiegelt anzeigen" in Settings > Kamera and in the dialog at switch-on decides how you see yourself. The others always get your picture unmirrored.

## Fixed
