## Improved

- **Your account key never reaches the app's page any more.** The key that stands for your account, and the keys of your server accounts, now stay in the app's own process. The page that shows the chat only asks it to sign and to open your direct messages and settings, and gets no key back. Should a flaw ever let foreign code into the page, it could act while the app is open, but it could not take your account along. On the first start the keys of the earlier version move over by themselves; you notice nothing of it. Works with every server version.
