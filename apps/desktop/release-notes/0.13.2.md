The channel dialog shows which permission entries you cannot change. The fix for setting channel permissions on servers imported from Discord lives on the server (Squorli Server 0.11.2).

## Improved

- **Entries above your rank are locked, with the reason.** In a channel's permissions, an entry for a role at or above your highest role shows a lock in place of the remove button, its switches are disabled, and a sentence says why. Until now every switch of the channel ran into "Diese Rolle steht über deiner." on such a server, whichever entry you changed.
