One fix for screen shares in fullscreen: the volume control of a stream you watch can be used again. Everything here lives in the app and works with every server version.

## Fixed

- **The volume control in fullscreen.** Since 0.17.2 the strip for switching streams along the bottom edge took the mouse before it reached the volume slider of the stream in fullscreen, so the slider could not be moved. The volume control now lies above that strip, in the stage and in a pop-out window.
