The app catches up with Squorli Server 0.15.1: switching streams in fullscreen, the own share shown black while the shared window is in front, and a stream you watch keeps you from being marked away. Everything here lives in the app and works with every server version.

## New

- **Switching streams in fullscreen.** While a screen share fills the screen, moving the mouse to the bottom edge shows the other streams that are running in the channel. A click switches to that one; fullscreen stays. Streams you have not turned on for yourself are switched on by that click.
- **The own share while the shared window is in front (Windows).** When the window or game you share is the one in front of you, your own share's tile and its pop-out window turn black with a notice that the preview is not rendered to save resources. The share itself keeps running; the picture comes back as soon as you switch to another window. A shared screen counts as in front while a program other than Squorli covers it whole.

## Improved

- **A stream you watch keeps you present.** A screen share in fullscreen, or a share pinned large in the speaker view, counts as activity: you are not marked away while you watch it (for at most four hours without any input, as with a playing video).
- **Fullscreen in the voice channel.** Fullscreen shows one tile of the stage, so the fullscreen button and Escape behave the same on every tile, and a tile that goes away leaves fullscreen by itself.
