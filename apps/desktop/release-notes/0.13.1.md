Roles made easier to read: a combination check in the administration, your own roles in the member menu, a red lock for channels you cannot enter. The fix for editing roles on servers imported from Discord lives on the server (Squorli Server 0.11.1).

## New

- **Kombination prüfen.** Under Verwaltung > Rollen, a button below the role list shows what a combination of roles adds up to: tick roles, or take a member's roles, and every right shows a check or an x with the roles that give it.
- **A red lock for a channel you cannot enter.** A voice channel you can see but may not join shows a red lock in place of the speaker.
- **Your own roles.** A right-click on your own name in the member list shows the roles you hold. Changing them is for somebody above you; owners still give themselves any role.

## Improved

- The channel dialog lists the roles in the hierarchy's order, as Verwaltung > Rollen does, with the default role last.
- The role editor locks the boxes of rights you do not hold yourself and says why, instead of refusing the save afterwards.
