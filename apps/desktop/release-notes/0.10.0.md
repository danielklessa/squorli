## New

- **Signing a device out now holds (Settings > Devices).** The list shows every device that is signed in with your account, where it signed in from (the site of a browser, or the desktop app), when it was signed in and last used, and which one you are sitting at. "Sign out" per device and "Sign out all other devices" ask for your password, and for a code while you have an authenticator. The device that was signed out falls back to the sign-in at once, loses your key and gets back in only with name, password and second factor. Until now it was back with one click. Needs servers with Squorli Server 0.7.0 or newer; a server with an older version still lets in whoever holds your key. For Squorli accounts, signing out is switched on at directory.squorli.com shortly after this version; until then the list shows and says so.
- **"Sign out" removes your key from this computer.** Signing out of the app signs this device out and takes your key and the keys of your server accounts along; name and password bring them back. For a Squorli account without a password the key exists on this computer only, so the app asks before it removes it.
- **At most ten devices per account.** At the eleventh, the sign-in shows the ten others after your password was right, and you pick the one that is signed out for it.
- **Devices that were not used for 90 days are signed out by themselves.**
- **A key for this device that cannot be read out.** The app makes it itself and keeps it where no script can copy it. Your account's key stays in the system's protected store as before.
