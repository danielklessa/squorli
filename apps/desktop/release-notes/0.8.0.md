## New

- **Block a member.** Right-click a member (or open their profile) and choose "Block", or block after reporting a message or a member. From then on that person's messages show folded ("Blocked message from …", with "Show" to open one), they do not mark a channel unread or count as a mention, and in a voice channel you no longer hear them. With a Squorli account the directory also refuses their friend requests. The blocked person is not told, and no server learns whom you block: with a Squorli account the list is kept encrypted in your account and holds on every server and device; a server account keeps it on this computer. Settings > Blocked lists whom you blocked and lifts a block. Blocking a friend works the same way and is lifted in the same place.
