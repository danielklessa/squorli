A fix for hearing someone who was already talking when you joined their channel and whom you had turned up above 100 %. Squorli Server 0.11.4 carries the same client for the browser.

## Fixed

- **Joining a channel where someone is already speaking.** Their voice arrived while the app was still setting up your own microphone, and a moment later the app dropped the level meter it had just made for them. Their speaker highlight then came from the server with a delay, and if you had set their volume above 100 %, the boost that hangs on that meter was gone with it: you heard nothing from them until they left and came back or you moved their slider. The meter now survives joining, with the boost on it.
