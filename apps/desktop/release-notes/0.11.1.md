## New

- **Privacy policy under the account forms.** Creating a server account shows a link to the server's privacy policy when its operator has entered one; creating a Squorli account inside the app links the Directory's privacy policy. The server's link needs Squorli Server 0.8.1 or newer.
- **Administration > Server: link to the privacy policy.** Owners and administrators enter the address of their privacy policy there. Needs Squorli Server 0.8.1 or newer; older servers show no such field.
