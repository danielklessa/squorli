A fix for servers whose structure came from Discord: roles and channel permissions can be edited again by everybody who may manage them. One rule changed: members no longer change their own roles. Desktop app 0.13.1 brings the client's part.

## Before you update

- **Nothing to do.** No database migration, no new variable: installations with automatic updates take this version by themselves once it is published.
- **One rule changed for members with "Rollen verwalten":** they no longer change their own roles, only those of members below them. A right-click on their own name still shows which roles they hold. Owners give themselves any role as before.
- **Desktop app:** 0.13.1 carries the client's part (the combination check, the red lock, the roles in the member menu, the order in the channel dialog). Older apps keep working with this server; the browser client that comes with the server has everything.

## New

- **Kombination prüfen.** Under Verwaltung > Rollen, a button below the role list shows what a combination of roles adds up to: tick roles, or take a member's roles, and every right shows a check or an x with the roles that give it. The default role counts for everybody.
- **A red lock for a channel you cannot enter.** A voice channel you can see but may not join shows a red lock in place of the speaker, whether it is private or not.
- **Your own roles in the member menu.** A right-click on your own name shows the roles you hold.

## Improved

- The channel dialog lists the roles in the hierarchy's order, as Verwaltung > Rollen does, with the default role last.
- The role editor locks the boxes of rights you do not hold yourself and says why, instead of refusing the save afterwards.

## Fixed

- **Roles and channel permissions on imported servers.** Whoever may manage roles got "cannot_grant" for every change of a role or of a channel's permissions as soon as that role or entry held one right they lacked themselves, not even a name or colour change got through. On a server imported from Discord that was nearly every role, the default role included. Now only the rights you change count; a right you do not hold stays as it is.
