Notifications, a choice about link previews in direct messages, the owner as a server account, and a camera blur that no longer loads anything from other sites.

## Before you update

- **No database migration, no newer Squorli Directory.** `squorli update` brings the new server.
- **Desktop app 0.8.1 follows after this server.** Older apps keep working; notifications, the stage's place holder, the list of who watches your share and the link preview switch reach app users with the new app, browser users with this update.
- **New, optional variable `OWNER_SETUP_CODE`:** only for a server without an owner yet (see New). Existing servers need nothing.
- **The installer asks who becomes the owner** (your Squorli account's key, a server account with a setup code, or whoever signs in first). It is downloaded fresh on every run; an existing installation needs nothing.

## New

- **The owner as a server account.** With `OWNER_SETUP_CODE` set (the installer makes one when you choose a server account), whoever registers a server account with that code becomes the owner; the registration form shows a field for it while there is no owner, also where server accounts are otherwise off. A name alone could be taken first by anybody, a code cannot.
- **Notifications.** Direct messages and mentions you do not see right now can show as notifications of the system; a click opens the conversation. In the browser switch them on under Settings > Sounds (the browser asks once, not on phones); the text can be left out.
- **Who watches your screen share.** The avatars of the people watching appear in the top left corner of your share, on the stage and in its own window; the pointer on one shows it large with the name.
- **Link previews in direct messages can be switched off** under Settings > View: no link is looked up, your messages go without previews, and you see none in the messages you receive. The choice follows your Squorli account, encrypted.

## Improved

- **The camera's background blur comes from your server.** Its model and program files ship with the client instead of being loaded from jsDelivr and Google; the page no longer allows scripts from any other site.
- **The stage in its own window keeps its place.** The main window shows where the stage went, with buttons to bring it back or to bring its window to the front.
- **The licenses list** (Settings > Licenses) names the desktop app's own packages and the blur model as well.

## Fixed

- **"Show the stage" did not bring the stage's window to the front** in the desktop app; it does with desktop app 0.8.1.
