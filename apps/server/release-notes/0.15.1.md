Three improvements around screen shares in voice channels: switching between streams without leaving fullscreen, the own share shown black while the shared window is in front (desktop app on Windows), and a stream you watch keeps you from being marked away. No migration, no new variable. Desktop app 0.17.2 follows with the same client.

## Before you update

- **Nothing to do by hand.** Installations with automatic updates take this version by themselves once it is published. There is no database migration and no new variable.
- **Desktop app:** 0.17.2 follows with the same client. Older apps keep working with this server; they just lack what is listed here. The browser client comes with the server, so members in a browser get it at once.

## New

- **Switching streams in fullscreen.** While a screen share fills the screen, moving the mouse to the bottom edge shows the other streams that are running in the channel. A click switches to that one; fullscreen stays. Streams you have not turned on for yourself are switched on by that click.
- **The own share while the shared window is in front (desktop app on Windows).** When the window or game you share is the one in front of you, your own share's tile and its pop-out window turn black with a notice that the preview is not rendered to save resources. The share itself keeps running; the picture comes back as soon as you switch to another window.

## Improved

- **A stream you watch keeps you present.** A screen share in fullscreen, or a share pinned large in the speaker view, counts as activity: you are not marked away while you watch it (for at most four hours without any input, as with a playing video).
- **Fullscreen in the voice channel.** Fullscreen is now the stage itself showing one tile, so the fullscreen button and Escape behave the same on every tile, and a tile that goes away leaves fullscreen by itself.
