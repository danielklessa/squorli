The radio's equalizer can be shown over the tiles, and on/off settings are toggle switches now. No migration and no new variable. Desktop app 0.14.3 brings the same client to the app.

## Before you update

- **Nothing to do:** no migration, no new variable, no change to the deploy files. Installations with automatic updates take this version by themselves once it is published.
- **Desktop app:** 0.14.3 carries the same client. Older apps keep working with this server; the browser client comes with the server. No newer Squorli Directory is needed.

## Improved

- **The equalizer over the tiles.** While the radio's equalizer is on, a switch right of its button in the stage's button bar draws it over the tiles instead of behind them. The tiles stay clickable. Off until a member turns it on; kept per device.
- **Toggle switches for on/off settings.** In the settings, in Administration and in the channel dialog every on/off setting is a switch now. Lists where several entries are ticked, such as a role's permissions, keep their checkboxes.
