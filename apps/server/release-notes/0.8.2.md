Security fixes for rate limits, roles, reports, kicks and bans, and links to the rules of use. Update soon.

## Before you update

- **This release contains security fixes.** Take it soon; the points under "Fixed" say what they are about.
- **Nothing to do by hand:** no database migration, no new or changed variable, no deploy file to fetch again. Installations with automatic updates take this version by themselves once it is published.
- **Desktop app:** older apps keep working. Two actions the server now refuses (giving a member a role with more permissions than you hold, and deleting messages through a report without the permission) are still offered by them and answer with an error; a new app version that hides them follows this release.
- No newer Squorli Directory is needed.

## Improved

- **The rules of use are linked** where they matter: in the notice a member gets about a moderator's measure, and under the form for a Squorli account inside the client ("By creating the account you accept the rules of use and the privacy policy"). Needs the desktop app that follows; the browser client has it with this release.
- **The role menu and the reports list show only what you may do.** A role you cannot hand out because it holds permissions you do not have is no longer offered, and the buttons that delete messages in a report need the permission to manage messages.

## Fixed

- **Rate limits** were skipped by some requests; they now apply to every request they are meant for.
- **Roles:** a member who may assign roles could give themselves a role with permissions they did not hold themselves. Assigning a role now needs the same permissions as editing it.
- **Reports:** closing a report with "delete" now needs the permission to manage messages in that channel, and deleting a person's recent messages needs a higher rank than that person, as banning does.
- **Kick, ban and account deletion** now also remove the person from the voice channel they are in; before, a modified client could stay in the room and keep listening.
- **A ban stays** when the banned person deletes their account.
- **Oversized messages** on the real-time connection (larger than 64 KiB) are refused.
